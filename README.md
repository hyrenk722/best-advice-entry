Entry for the make.fun competition "The Best Advice I Ever Got": one sentence, up to 200 characters, about the best advice you ever received
`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating the gate first, then reading
the file back to verify the written bytes match.

> My mother said marry someone whose bad days you can stand, because the good days take care of themselves.

Why this entry: A real voice recalling a real moment: a mother, a marriage test, no slogan. The advice is specific and earned (judge bad days, not good ones), true beyond the moment, and it lands with a quiet turn — the good days take care of themselves. One sentence, one breath, 105 characters.
